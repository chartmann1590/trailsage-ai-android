# Attribution

Sample tour notes link to Wikipedia contributors under CC BY-SA. Map content is derived from OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL. Production image packs must retain Wikimedia Commons author, source URL, and license metadata. Gemma, LiteRT-LM, Sherpa-ONNX, and downloaded voice packs retain their upstream terms.

## Bundled in-app imagery

The hero/destination artwork bundled in the app (`app/src/main/res/drawable/hero_forest.xml`, `hero_coast.xml`, `hero_desert.xml`) is original stylized vector illustration created for TrailSage AI and released as CC0 (public domain). No third-party photography is bundled. Tour packs may additionally supply Wikimedia Commons photos, which are loaded with their own attribution from each pack's `attribution.json` and surfaced on the in-app Attribution screen.
