App package: com.charles.trailsage

DATA COLLECTED / SHARED
- Location (approximate & precise): Used ON-DEVICE only for GPS-triggered narration
  and routing. NOT sent off-device, NOT sold, NOT shared. Declare as "not collected"
  off the device (processing stays local).
- Diagnostics / Crash logs (Firebase): OPTIONAL, OFF by default. If a user opts in,
  anonymous diagnostics may be collected. Declare as collected (optional) when enabled.
- Advertising (Google AdMob): The app shows ads. AdMob may collect device/advertising
  identifiers for ad delivery and measurement. Declare AdMob data collection/sharing
  per Google's AdMob guidance. An optional rewards-based ad-free tier is available.

THIRD-PARTY SDKs PRESENT
- Google AdMob (ads)
- Firebase (optional, opt-in diagnostics)

NOT PRESENT
- No accounts/login required
- No sale of personal data
- Trip history, routes, and AI queries are not transmitted off the device

IMPORTANT: The marketing copy must NOT claim "no ads" or "zero telemetry" in absolute
terms because AdMob is integrated and optional Firebase diagnostics exist. Keep copy
