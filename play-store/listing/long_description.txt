TrailSage AI is your private, offline road trip storyteller.

Designed for deep mountain valleys, scenic highways, and remote national parks where cellular service vanishes, TrailSage AI packages on-device Artificial Intelligence, expressive neural narration, and detailed offline maps to tell you the stories of the land as you drive.

🌟 KEY FEATURES

🧠 LOCAL ON-DEVICE AI
* Powered by Google Gemma 2B and on-device LiteRT.
* Ask questions about surrounding landmarks, local history, geography, or flora and fauna.
* Works completely offline—no cloud APIs, cellular data, or internet connection required.
* Utilizes a localized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system for accurate, context-aware responses.

🗣️ NATURAL NEURAL VOICES
* Driven by the Sherpa-ONNX synthesis engine and VITS voice profiles.
* Experience highly expressive, natural human voice tours instead of robotic system text-to-speech fallbacks.
* Choose from multiple voice styles to fit the mood of your adventure.

🗺️ OFFLINE OPENSTREETMAPS
* Explore with fully offline vector maps powered by PMTiles extracts.
* Track paths, trails, roads, and topography with zero latency.
* Seamless navigation HUD designed specifically for driving.

📍 GPS-TRIGGERED NARRATION
* Advanced radius and bearing calculations automatically play stories.
* Hear about historical events and points of interest the exact second you approach a landmark from the correct direction.
* Curated and customizable tour packages.

🔗 ANONYMOUS TRIP SHARING
* Create and share custom itineraries with friends.
* Share links that preview in any web browser with interactive maps, summaries, and directions.
* Import trips directly via deep links.

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN
* Your trip history, routes, and location coordinates stay on your device—never sold or shared.
* On-device AI and narration mean your questions and travels never leave your phone.
* Optional, anonymous diagnostics (Firebase) are OFF by default—you choose whether to share them.
* Supported by non-intrusive ads, with an optional rewards-based ad-free tier.

How to start:
1. Load your tour pack (e.g., Adirondack High Peaks Loop) to download offline maps, routes, and story assets.
2. Follow on-device setup prompts to verify the local Gemma LLM.
3. Install your preferred Piper/VITS voice pack.
4. Turn on GPS, tap "Start Driving," and let the story unfold!
