What's the history of that old stone bridge?

psychology

That’s the Miller's Crossing Bridge, built in 1892. It was constructed by local stonemasons using granite quarried just two miles upstream.

Originally designed for horse-drawn timber wagons, it survived the Great Flood of 1927 when most other wooden structures in the valley were washed away. It’s considered a prime example of late 19th-century rusticated stone arch construction.
