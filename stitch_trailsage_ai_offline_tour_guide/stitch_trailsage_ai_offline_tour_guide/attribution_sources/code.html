Our Data Philosophy

TrailSage AI is built on the incredible work of global open-source communities. We synthesize public geographic data, historical records, and crowd-sourced imagery to power our audio guides. We believe in transparency and deeply respect the licenses that make this platform possible.

How AI Processes This Data

TrailSage uses language models to summarize and format this public data into conversational audio clips suitable for driving or hiking. The AI does not train on your personal location data. It merely requests context for the coordinates you are actively exploring.
